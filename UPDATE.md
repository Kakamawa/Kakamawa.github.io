# Website maintenance

Update the site when a new journal article, preprint, conference record, affiliation/degree change, or substantial public research project becomes public.

## Monthly check
- Search for newly public, verified records attributable to Kartik Mawa.
- Check topic-only searches around H2, CO2, geothermal, EGS, reservoir simulation, and EDFM.
- Add only accurate public material.
- Update the last-updated date.

## Every 6 months
- Verify Google Scholar, ORCID, LinkedIn, and institutional profile links.
- Check old links and remove stale material.
- Rerun the topic-only discoverability checks.

Principle: one canonical page + accurate scholarly records + legitimate public versions. No keyword stuffing, fake citations, or manufactured references.
